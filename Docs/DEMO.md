# Show LibreReplace in 45 seconds

**Story:** one paid app, several possible free tools, one setup you can come back to.

## The demo

1. **Discover:** select CleanShot X and choose Screenshots and OCR text capture. Enable LibreReplace's on-device image OCR.
2. **See the free stack:** show a combination of a free capture app and LibreReplace Image OCR, with the coverage summary visible.
3. **Explore:** open the setup and show that it recognizes what's available on this Mac. Keep optional installation collapsed.
4. **Try a real tool:** open image text extraction, choose a harmless screenshot containing a few lines of text and extract it.
5. **Return:** click Return to setup and show the original requirements and components still there.
6. **Save:** visit My Setups and reopen the saved combination.

## What viewers should remember

**LibreReplace doesn't just find alternative apps. It helps you build, test and keep an actual free workflow.**

Record the real app window and real sample output. The best demo is the one that makes people think, *“I could use that today.”*
