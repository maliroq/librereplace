# Roadmap

LibreReplace is in preview stabilization. Product scope is intentionally frozen while the existing scanner, catalog, ranking, installation wiring, migration safety, documentation, and release process are exercised by real users.

## Preview priorities

- correct reproducible bugs;
- correct catalog evidence, bundle identifiers, licenses, installation paths, and limitations;
- keep evidence current;
- improve tests when a real failure or safety invariant is identified;
- fix accessibility or usability defects without expanding the product surface;
- complete signing/notarization work for binary distribution.

## Not commitments

Future targets, additional migration adapters, update channels, or other product expansion should be driven by demonstrated user needs after the preview. They are deliberately not promised here.

## Explicitly out of scope for the preview

- automatic source-app uninstallation or data deletion;
- credential/Keychain/browser migration;
- accounts, telemetry, advertising, or cloud inventory;
- automatically generated recommendation claims;
- automatic scraping/classification of unsupported apps.
