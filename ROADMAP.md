# What's next for LibreReplace

LibreReplace's direction is simple: **make it easier to replace a paid Mac app with a free setup that really fits the work you do.**

## The next big milestone: Studio in everyone's hands

- Package and distribute the new five-section Studio experience as a downloadable macOS app.
- Complete the final clean-install, artwork and macOS distribution checks.
- Refresh the gallery of real app screenshots and demo materials.
- Bring the first reviewed Community setups into the gallery.

## Keep making the core experience better

- **More complete setups:** practical combinations built around real tasks instead of a one-app-fits-all assumption.
- **Better bridges:** more focused built-in utilities that make an alternative immediately useful.
- **Smoother switching:** additional well-tested migrations for settings and supported workflows.
- **Richer Community recipes:** reproducible setups that people can import, inspect and adapt.
- **Better accessibility and polish:** keep every step clear, keyboard-friendly and at home on macOS.

## What makes the product different

The priority isn't the biggest catalog or the most features. It's the **best path from “I pay for this app” to “I have a setup I actually like.”** New capabilities should make that path shorter, clearer or more reliable.
